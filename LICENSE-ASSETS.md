This file covers everything else that carries GhostDeck's visual identity:

- `app.ico` and the icon embedded in the executable
- `assets/icons/*.svg` (the ghost mark and the profile icons)
- the banners, logos and other project artwork under `docs/`

otherwise.

## What you may do

- **Redistribute them unchanged as part of an unmodified GhostDeck release.** Mirroring or
  packaging an official build does not require asking.
- **Keep them in a copy of the source tree**, including a fork, for building and reference.
- **Use them in articles, reviews, documentation and screenshots** about GhostDeck.
- **Say that your work is derived from GhostDeck**, for example "based on GhostDeck" or
  "fork of GhostDeck", as a plain statement of fact.

## What you may not do

- **Use them as the identity of a modified version.** If you distribute a modified GhostDeck,
  remove or replace the icon, the logo and the artwork.
- **Present a modified version as official**, or name it in a way that suggests it is, such as
  "GhostDeck Pro" or "GhostDeck Plus".
- **Use them as the logo or icon of a different product.**

The GPL gives you the right to modify and redistribute the code. It does not give you the right
to ship your version wearing this project's face. Those are separate things, and keeping them
separate is what lets users tell an official build from someone else's.

The name **GhostDeck** is covered separately in [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md).

## Icon fonts

The interface also draws glyphs from Segoe MDL2 Assets and Segoe Fluent Icons, the icon fonts
installed with Windows. Those are Microsoft's and are used, not redistributed: no font file
ships with GhostDeck.

## Questions

If you want to do something this file does not clearly allow, open an issue and ask. The
intention here is narrow: stop a modified build from impersonating an official one. It is not
to make writing about GhostDeck, packaging it, or forking it awkward.
